What are the key differences between the Pro and Enterprise pricing tiers?✓ PASS
9407 ms · $0.00127
answer
**Pro vs. Enterprise:** Pro has fixed prices and limits. Enterprise is custom-priced with higher limits and extra features. Each product's differences are below.

**Nimbus Relay** (per seat per month, annual plan)
- Pro: $49 [1]. See the pricing note below.
- Enterprise: Custom [1].
- Seats: Pro 25; Enterprise Unlimited [1].
- API rate limit: Pro 10,000 req/min; Enterprise 100,000 req/min [1].
- Single sign-on: Pro No; Enterprise SAML 2.0 [1].
- Audit log retention: Pro 90 days; Enterprise 400 days [1].
- Request replay: Pro No; Enterprise Yes [1].
- Regional routing: Pro US only; Enterprise US and EU [1].
- Support plan: Pro Standard; Enterprise Premium [1].

⚠️ **Documents disagree:** The Pro price is $49 per seat per month in relay.md (updated 2026-06-12) [1], but $59 per seat per month in relay-release-notes.md (4.2, 2026-06-10) [5], for new contracts signed on or after 1 August 2026.
- Applies today (2026-10-07): the $59 price applies to new contracts signed on or after 1 August 2026. Existing contracts keep their price until renewal [5].

**Nimbus Vault** (per seat per month, annual plan)
- Pro: $35; Enterprise: Custom [2].
- Secrets: Pro 10,000; Enterprise Unlimited [2].
- Single sign-on: Pro SAML 2.0; Enterprise SAML 2.0 [2].
- HSM-backed keys: Pro No; Enterprise Yes [2].
- Audit log retention: Pro 180 days; Enterprise 400 days [2].
- Support plan: Pro Standard; Enterprise Premium [2].

**Nimbus Pulse** (per workspace per month, annual plan)
- Pro: $299; Enterprise: Custom [3].
- Monthly events: Pro 50 million; Enterprise Custom [3].
- Session replay: Pro Yes; Enterprise Yes [3].
- Warehouse export: Pro Snowflake; Enterprise Snowflake and BigQuery [3].
- Support plan: Pro Standard; Enterprise Premium [3].

**Nimbus Ledger** (per month, annual plan, plus 0.5% of invoiced volume)
- Pro: $199; Enterprise: Custom [4].
- Invoiced volume included: Pro $500,000; Enterprise Custom [4].
- Federated login (SAML 2.0): Pro Yes; Enterprise Yes [4].
- Dunning: Pro Yes; Enterprise Yes [4].
- Support plan: Pro Standard; Enterprise Premium [4].

A client is getting a 403 on the API. What should they check first?✓ PASS
3023 ms · $0.00044
answer
**Nimbus Relay** [1]
1. **(check first)** The token's scope includes the route being called. Scopes are per route since Relay 4.0; a token minted for `/orders` cannot call `/customers`.
2. The caller's IP is on the workspace allowlist, if one is configured.
3. The seat that owns the token is not suspended.

**Nimbus Pulse** [3]
1. **(check first)** The user who owns the API key is a member of the workspace the project belongs to.
2. The API key is bound to the project being queried; keys are per project since 4.0.

**Nimbus Vault and Nimbus Ledger:** not in the NimbusStack knowledge base. The knowledge base has Vault integration requirements [2], but no 403 troubleshooting for Vault.

Which of our products support SSO via SAML 2.0?✓ PASS
5143 ms · $0.00085
answer
**Products with SAML 2.0 SSO:**

- **Nimbus Ledger:** SAML 2.0 on every tier [4][5][6].
- **Nimbus Relay:** SAML 2.0 on Enterprise [1][5].
- **Nimbus Vault:** SAML 2.0 on Pro and Enterprise [2]. Starter supports password-only sign-in [2].

⚠️ **Documents disagree:** On Nimbus Vault, [5] security-overview.md (2026-01-15) says "SAML 2.0 on Enterprise," while [2] vault.md (2026-07-03) says "SAML 2.0" on Pro and Enterprise. Release notes [8] vault-release-notes.md (2026-04-14) also say SAML 2.0 was "extended to the Pro tier (previously Enterprise only)."
Today, SAML 2.0 applies to Vault Pro and Enterprise. The security overview predates the 2026-04-14 change, and the newer vault.md matches the release notes.

**Nimbus Pulse: no SAML 2.0.** Pulse supports single sign-on through OpenID Connect (OIDC) on Pro and Enterprise, tested with Okta, Google Workspace and Microsoft Entra [3][5]. SAML 2.0 is on the roadmap and is not available today [3].
