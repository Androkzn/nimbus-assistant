1393 ms · $0.00032
answer
**Nimbus Relay · 403 Forbidden on an API call:** check these in order:

1. **check first:** The token's scope includes the route being called. Scopes are per route since Relay 4.0; a token minted for `/orders` cannot call `/customers` [1].
2. The caller's IP is on the workspace allowlist, if one is configured [1].
3. The seat that owns the token is not suspended [1].

What are the key differences between Vault Pro and Enterprise?✓ PASS
6121 ms · $0.00095
answer
**Key differences between Vault Pro and Enterprise:**

- **Price:** Pro is $35 per seat per month; Enterprise is Custom. Prices are per seat per month on an annual plan [2].
- **Secrets:** Pro allows 10,000; Enterprise is Unlimited [2].
- **HSM-backed keys:** Not available on Pro; available on Enterprise [2]. These were added on Enterprise in 3.1 [4][1].
- **Audit log retention:** Pro keeps 180 days; Enterprise keeps 400 days [2].
- **Support plan:** Pro gets Standard; Enterprise gets Premium [2].
- **Support response time to first human reply** (not resolution time) [3]:

| Priority | Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|
| P1 (service down) | 1 hour | 30 minutes, 24x7 |
| P2 (degraded) | 4 hours | 2 hours |
| P3 (question) | 1 business day | 8 hours |
| P4 (feature request) | Best effort | 5 business days |

⚠️ **Documents disagree:** Pro single sign-on is SAML 2.0 in vault.md (updated 2026-07-03) [2] and in vault-release-notes.md, version 3.1 (2026-04-14), which extended SAML 2.0 to Pro [1]. The security-overview.md (updated 2026-01-15) lists Nimbus Vault as SAML 2.0 on Enterprise only [5]. The two later documents, dated 2026-04-14 and 2026-07-03, both say Pro has SAML 2.0, and the security overview predates the 3.1 change, so the later documents reflect current status.

Both tiers support SAML 2.0 sign-in according to the pricing table [2], and Enterprise and Pro both have access policies per team and per application [4] (this is listed as a general feature, not a tier-specific one).
